Filter chain

Shape the clip before it leaves.

Build an ordered chain with crop, rotation, vertical flip, frame rate, contrast and color balance. Crop stays synchronized between numeric margins and draggable on-picture handles.

Just the person, at the size they were. Follow person finds their own window or split-screen panel in every camera shot and leaves other guests, footage and background out. Dynamic keeps every shot; landscape, portrait or square keep only shots of that shape. Nothing is enlarged, and the HEVC result is usually far smaller than the broadcast it came from.
It refuses to lose your footage. Every automatic result is checked for duration and decoded at its end before success. Failed, cancelled, partial and unreviewed jobs keep their source. Free and open source under MIT. Download for Windows, Linux and macOS.
